---
name: Soft Rituals visual direction
description: Durable product direction for future Soft Rituals improvements.
---

Soft Rituals should retain its existing editorial storefront identity, including its branding, colors, typography, layout, and overall aesthetic. Improvements should focus on clarity, accessibility, motion, performance, and small UX refinements rather than redesigning the page.

**Why:** The owner explicitly wants the current design preserved and considers the site’s visual identity part of the product.

**How to apply:** Treat future requests as polish passes unless the owner explicitly asks for a redesign. Keep product availability messaging clear: Sunday Reset Planner is the only currently purchasable item; other products and bundles are coming soon.
